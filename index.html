About Me

Hi! I’m Muskan Gupta. I’m a Technical Artist who loves bringing art and technology together to create real-time experiences.

I’m originally from New Delhi and currently based in Bengaluru, with 6+ years of experience across Technical Art, real-time VFX, shaders, 3D production, and game development. I’ve grown through progressive roles at Zynga, from Associate Technical Artist to Technical Artist 2, where I’ve had the opportunity to work on mobile games, new IP, and LiveOps.

What I enjoy most about Technical Art is solving the problems behind the visuals. Whether it’s building tools that make artists’ lives easier, creating VFX and shaders, optimizing assets and rendering performance, or improving production pipelines, I like finding practical ways to make both the creative process and the final experience better.

My work spans Unity, C#, Python, JavaScript, HLSL, Maya, Substance 3D, and other DCC tools. I enjoy working closely with artists, designers, and engineers, turning creative ideas into polished, efficient, and production-ready experiences.

For me, Technical Art is about finding the sweet spot between how something looks and how well it works — and making sure the two work beautifully together.
